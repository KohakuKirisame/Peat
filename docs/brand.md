Create a compact abstract peat-cut silhouette with two or three organic sediment strata and a restrained sea-salt colored cut through it. Style: exceptionally clean vector-like flat symbol, bold readable silhouette, refined irregular geometry, earthy dark peat brown and muted coastal sage. Centered single logo only, suitable at favicon scale, no text, no wordmark, no letters, no border, no presentation sheet, no mockup, no shadows, no gradients. Real transparent background.
